## Brand & Style

This design system blends ancient Korean metaphysics (사주명리 · Saju Myungri) with refined modern mobile minimalism. The aesthetic bridges celestial reverence and contemporary editorial prestige. It rejects mystical tropes (such as garish talismanic yellows or chaotic folk motifs) in favor of deep midnight stillness, restrained champagne gold calligraphy, translucent obsidian glass, and balanced astronomical geometry.

The target audience consists of design-conscious millennials and Gen Z seekers in South Korea who view fortune-telling through an analytical, psychological, and self-reflective lens. The interface evokes a sense of tranquil discovery: reading one's cosmic blueprint should feel like viewing an astral chart inside an exclusive, moonlit observatory.

Visual language leans into dark-mode glassmorphism structured by fine, hairline metallic borders, luminous elemental jewel tones, and calm, spacious typography.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in Korean cosmological philosophy (음양오행 · Yin-Yang and the Five Elements), calibrated strictly for low-strain night viewing and luxurious clarity.

### Base Canvases & Glass
- **Canvas Base (Deep Obsidian Navy):** `#0B0F19` as the root screen background, transitioning to `#111827` and `#161F30` for subtle atmospheric radial blooms.
- **Glass Surfaces:** Semi-translucent dark slate navy (`rgba(28, 37, 56, 0.72)` and `rgba(34, 46, 70, 0.60)`) backed with background blur.
- **Surface Borders:** Delicate metallic gold hairline stroke (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)` idle, `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.65)` active/highlighted).

### Accents & Gold Spectrum
- **Champagne Gold (Brand Primary):** `#D4AF37` for key actions, sacred glyphs, and high-tier fortune ratings.
- **Celestial Amber / Radiance:** `#F59E0B` and `#E6CA65` for gradient shimmer, highlights, and favorable astrological pillars.

### The Five Elements (오행 · Oh-Haeng) Palette
Functional elemental tokens must be applied strictly to their respective stems, branches, and balance charts:
- **Wood (목 / 木 - Blue/Green):** `#10B981` (Vibrant Emerald) / `#06B6D4` (Cyan Teal). Represents growth, birth, and springtime vitality.
- **Fire (화 / 火 - Red):** `#F43F5E` (Crimson Rose) / `#FB7185` (Coral Glow). Represents passion, expansion, and celestial sun.
- **Earth (토 / 土 - Yellow):** `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber Ochre) / `#D97706`. Represents stability, mediation, and center.
- **Metal (금 / 金 - White):** `#E2E8F0` (Luminous Pearl Silver) / `#F8FAFC`. Represents harvest, sharp intellect, and clarity.
- **Water (수 / 水 - Black/Deep Blue):** `#3B82F6` (Electric Azure) / `#1E293B` (Abyssal Slate). Represents wisdom, intuition, and depth.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative editorial balance between literary antiquity and clinical data readability. 

- **Display & Headings (Noto Serif KR fallback to Noto Serif):** Used for poetic titles, divination summaries, the user's primary Day Master (일간 · Ilgan), and seasonal transitions. Best paired with champagne gold text fills or high-contrast silver (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Body & Data Density (Noto Sans KR fallback to Noto Sans):** Optimized for high-density analysis cards, pillar breakdowns (사주 4기둥), and explanatory fortune prose. Clear vertical line-heights prevent dense Hangul glyphs from clustering.
- **Elemental Glyphs & Micro Data (Space Grotesk):** Applied to alphanumeric birth charts, coordinates, celestial time stamps, and uppercase categorization badges, giving an astrological-laboratory precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built primarily for a fluid mobile viewport (360px–430px canvas width) with structured single-column cascading cards.

- **Canvas Margins:** Set to `1.25rem` (20px) to maximize touch real estate while maintaining an intentional frame around midnight-glass containers.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component modules sit on an 8pt spatial grid (`space-sm` for stacked sub-details, `space-lg` between distinct astrological cards, and `space-xl` prior to primary section breaks).
- **The Four Pillars (사주팔자) Matrix:** Uses a strict 4-column balanced grid with `gutter` (`1rem`) to present the Year (연), Month (월), Day (일), and Hour (시) columns with equal visual weight and symmetrical harmony.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use heavy opaque drop shadows. Depth is realized through physical translucency, cosmic atmospheric glows, and precise metallic strokes.

- **Elevation Level 0 (Ground Canvas):** Pitch-black navy `#0B0F19` with ambient radial color bleeds (`rgba(22, 31, 48, 0.45)`).
- **Elevation Level 1 (Primary Glass Cards):** Background fill `rgba(28, 37, 56, 0.65)` layered over a `16px` backdrop filter blur. Outlined by a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Floating Modals & Interactive Nodes):** Fill `rgba(34, 46, 70, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop blur, subtle top-down directional gradient border (`rgba(230, 202, 101, 0.35)` to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.05)`), and a soft celestial aura shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Elemental Focus Glows:** When an element is spotlighted (e.g., active Wood energy), the card gains an inner rim ambient cast corresponding to that element's signature hue at `0.15` opacity.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curvature (Option 2: Rounded). 

- Standard cards, interactive sheets, and chart wrappers use `0.75rem` to `1rem` radius, balancing the stern geometry of classical trigrams with mobile-native touch friendliness.
- Elemental tokens and pillar tags maintain structured pill silhouettes (`9999px`) to echo the spherical orbit of celestial bodies.
- Micro-dividers and seasonal fortune gauges utilize delicate tapered endpoints rather than harsh square terminations.

## Components

### Glassmorphic Fortune Cards
- **Structure:** `1rem` corner radius, `rgba(28, 37, 56, 0.7)` backdrop, `1px` stroke using `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- **Header:** Features a Korean title in Noto Serif with an accompanying miniature gold astrological seal or celestial coordinate in Space Grotesk.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Gradient fill from `#D4AF37` to `#F59E0B`, deep midnight text (`#0B0F19`), font weight `600`. Outer glow of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Glass Button:** Translucent slate navy fill, `1px` champagne border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`), gold or pearl text, zero shadow.
- **Pill Tab Switches:** Segmented control embedded in a dark recessed track (`#111827`), active state transitions to an obsidian card with subtle gold trim.

### Elemental Fortune Meter & Circular Gauges
- **Gauge Ring:** Concentric SVG segmented tracks depicting the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) in their respective neon-refined jewel tones.
- **Center Value:** Displays overall divine compatibility or daily cosmic score in champagne gold Noto Serif, paired with an elemental archetype glyph (e.g., 陽木).

### Four Pillars (사주원국) Data Grid
- **Pillar Cell:** Vertical card divided into Heavenly Stem (천간 · top) and Earthly Branch (지지 · bottom).
- **Hanzi / Hangul Badge:** High-contrast elemental background fill (e.g., Fire cell has dark crimson background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)` with `#FB7185` text and 1px elemental border).
- **Labels:** Tiny Space Grotesk captions denoting Year, Month, Day, and Hour along the header.

### Inputs & Date Selection
- **Field Appearance:** Obsidian glass input well with recessed depth, placeholder in muted slate (`#64748B`), and active state shifting border stroke to glowing champagne gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Solar/Lunar (양력/음력) Toggle:** Sleek dual-pill segmented switch with smooth state animation.

### Navigation Bar
- **Bottom Bar:** Frosted obsidian glass dock (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.88)` with `20px` blur), top hairline border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Navigation Icons:** Fine-line celestial geometry (astrolabe, compass, solar disk, trigram book). Active icon glows in gold with a micro dot beneath.
